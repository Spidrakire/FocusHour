# Focus Hour: participant FAQ and operating rules (draft)

**Status:** internal draft. Not published. Confirm any policy changes before public use.

## When does Focus Hour take place?
Every Monday, 6.30–7.45pm UK time. The UK time zone adjusts automatically between GMT and BST.

## What happens during a session?
10 minutes to set a goal, 60 minutes of quiet independent work alongside the group and 5 minutes to share progress.

## How does the free trial work?
Your first **four consecutive Monday sessions** are free, starting with the Monday you select when registering. The four dates are fixed even if you cannot attend one of them.

## Do I need to enter payment details?
No. There are no payment details required to register for the free trial.

## What happens after the free trial?
You can choose to continue for **£19 per month**. There is no obligation to subscribe and no automatic payment should begin without a separate, explicit payment authorisation.

## Can I join on the same day?
On Mondays, registration for that evening remains available until 6.29.59pm UK time. At 6.30pm, the earliest available start becomes the following Monday.

## How do I join?
After a successful registration, joining instructions must be available immediately and sent in an essential welcome email. The secure link and delivery mechanism are still being implemented and must be tested before launch.

## What if I miss a session?
The four trial sessions are consecutive calendar Mondays, regardless of attendance. A missed session does not extend the free trial.

## Will I receive marketing emails?
Only if you choose the separate marketing opt-in. Essential messages about sessions, joining details and operational changes are independent of that choice.

## Can I cancel a paid membership?
The website states that you can cancel whenever you like. The cancellation method and any payment-provider billing rules need to be documented before taking payments.

## Operating checklist

### Before inviting participants
- Confirm secure joining URL management and access controls.
- Verify immediate confirmation, essential welcome email and session reminders.
- Confirm all four trial dates are calculated from the chosen first Monday.
- Verify contact synchronisation to Brevo list 6 and retry/error recovery.
- Establish how the organiser will see registrations and attendees for each Monday.
- Define the process for handling a missing joining email, an incorrect registration date or a late arrival.
- Confirm the paid membership and cancellation process before accepting payments.

### Each Monday
- Check upcoming eligible attendees and registration submissions.
- Check any failed CRM synchronisations and essential email deliveries.
- Ensure joining instructions are available to same-day registrants before 6.30pm.
- Start the 75-minute session at 6.30pm UK time.

### After the session
- Reconcile new registrations against Netlify Forms and Brevo.
- Check that trial eligibility advances by calendar date rather than attendance.
- Do not automatically enrol trial participants into a paid plan.
