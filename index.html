Why Focus Hour?

Most of us know exactly what would improve our lives.

Start the business. Apply for the new role. Grow your client pipeline. Learn a new skill. Improve your finances. Finish the course. Create something meaningful.

The problem isn't knowing what to do. It's finding the time, space and commitment to actually do it.

01

The important things rarely feel urgent.

The things that improve our lives usually don't have deadlines. So they quietly slip down the list while work, clients, family, admin and everyday life take over.

Weeks become months. The important things stay important. They just never become urgent enough.

Focus Hour gives those things the time they deserve.

02

Good intentions aren't enough.

Most of us have a growing list of things we want to do, need to do or feel we should do. When everything feels important, it's difficult to decide where to start.

We choose the easier things and leave the harder, scarier or more meaningful ones for another day.

Except tomorrow becomes next week. Next week becomes next month.

03

You don't need to do everything.

A never ending to do list can make even important things start to feel like obligations. Focus Hour removes that pressure.

During your session, you don't need to think about everything. You just need to decide what's most important today.

04

You're great at keeping promises to other people.

Clients. Managers. Family. Friends. Appointments. Deadlines. You show up because people are depending on you.

Focus Hour helps you keep one promise that's easy to overlook. The promise you made to yourself.
